Organization (fictional example)

People

Harbor Pine Logistics is a fictional 40-person company used for every scenario in this lab. Its people sponsor work and hold the review role: a task reaches Done only when a person-side reviewer who is not the assignee approves it. Reviewer choices in this demo are drawn from the Digital Employee roster to keep the instrument self-contained; the rule enforced is the paper’s: reviewer ≠ assignee.

Digital Employees

DH-002 Specialist · research and drafting
DH-004 Operator · operations and execution
DH-006 Reviewer · review and compliance
Each works only inside MeshGroups whose approved resource list covers the task, and only against that group’s pooled capacity.

MeshGroups

Admission is pooled-capacity accounting over the group’s approved resources only. Node placement is the MeshStack dispatch layer and is out of scope for admission; nothing here splits, borrows, or reroutes demand.
