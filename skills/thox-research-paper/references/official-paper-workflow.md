# Official THOX paper workflow baseline

This bundled baseline makes the workflow executable when a separately installed research-publishing standard is not exposed. It consolidates the existing paper structure and the requested Research Studio stages. Prefer a verified newer canonical THOX research standard when available and record its revision. If only this baseline is available, identify it as `THOX Research Team 1.2.0 baseline`; do not claim another package or its independent validation was loaded.

## Inputs and authors

Resolve the actual topic, research question, contribution, intended audience, available evidence and desired formats from the request and current project. Creating or updating this plugin alone does not start a paper. Load the shared publication-author profile and include both Craig Ross and Tommy Xaypanya by default, preserving approved order and verified additional coauthors. Keep account details private. Human authors remain responsible for the work; record real contributions, consent and AI assistance before submission.

## Seven evidence-gated stages

1. **Intake:** identify canonical repositories, source revisions, project ID, existing work and publication phase. Record whether the manuscript is experimental, theoretical, a review, an evaluation protocol or a proposed architecture. Select one integrator and assign bounded literature, methods, engineering, technical-review and artifact-review responsibilities to available executors. Do not label the same executor's second pass independent review.
2. **Literature:** search topic-relevant primary CS/AI papers from the preceding six calendar months, expanding to twelve months and justified adjacent fields. Respect an explicit window. Separate first-public and revision dates; retain older foundations explicitly. Verify bibliographic metadata, full-text claims, limitations and relevant code. Build a methods/comparison matrix and a separate structure/presentation matrix. Reading papers supplies context, not model-weight training, and does not license copying their prose.
3. **Outline:** specify a falsifiable question, intended contribution, comparison baselines, metrics, resource conditions, assumptions and failure criteria. Link each planned claim to needed evidence. Do not invent novelty or select metrics solely because they favor the proposal.
4. **Draft and audit:** write original prose and implement authorized experiments against pinned revisions. Retain commands, environment, seeds where applicable, raw outputs and failures. Label every material result as external literature, observed, simulated, calculated, proposed or unverified. Do not report simulated outcomes as device measurements. Attach citations and evidence IDs to claims and figures.
5. **Refine:** address concrete reviewer findings, re-run affected experiments and re-audit changed claims. Preserve negative results. Limit each bounded run to five refinement cycles, then retain unresolved blockers and a concrete handoff rather than claiming completion or an indefinite background loop.
6. **Validate:** check calculations, source attribution, baseline fairness, citations, references, argument strength, limitations and evidence completeness. Use a genuinely independent reviewer when available; otherwise record that gate as pending. Render applicable output formats and inspect every page for missing content, broken equations, overflows and stale metadata. A structural test, generated document or successful save is not scientific validation.
7. **Handoff:** freeze matching versions of the manuscript, bibliography, figures/source, experiment records, claim ledger, review findings, preflight report and private checkpoint. Return actual artifacts and receipts, list unresolved limitations and distinguish draft, validated, authorized, uploaded, submitted and announced. Do not submit from a drafting request.

## Standard manuscript structure

Title; verified human authors and affiliations; Abstract (normally 150-250 words unless the target venue differs); Introduction with contributions; Related Work; Method or Architecture; Evaluation Protocol; Experiments and Results or explicitly labeled Proposed Evaluation; Discussion and Limitations; Conclusion; References; reproducibility/ethics/AI-assistance disclosures and appendices as applicable.

Do not fabricate an Experiments and Results section when no measurements exist. Theoretical work uses checkable derivations. A literature review distinguishes reported external findings from THOX results. Data and code availability statements must reflect actual authorized availability. Do not release private code to make a paper look reproducible.

## Official deliverables and presentation

Produce the requested formats, not every possible format. For a complete paper request, prepare editable manuscript/source, verified bibliography, figures with generation source and captions, evidence/claim ledger, reproducibility instructions, validation findings and a private handoff. Generate a PDF only when a renderer is available and inspect it; otherwise report that render validation remains pending.

Use the installed `thox-official-templates` native masters for branded THOX whitepapers, reports and handoffs, after reading that skill and its actual template defaults. Use current brand-governance instructions when available. Keep a separate scholarly export conforming to the target venue; do not add promotional panels or slogans to the scholarly manuscript. Put job titles in a venue-compatible author biography or title block and preserve the human authors and affiliations in all appropriate metadata fields. Do not bundle or distribute font binaries.

## arXiv boundary

Read `arxiv-preflight.md` and `arxiv-account-workflow.md`. Require source for TeX-authored work, a clean staging folder, complete references/dependencies and source-level archival review. Revalidate the exact files after any change and inspect the server-generated PDF after an authorized upload. Refresh live platform rules rather than hard-coding submission quotas. Publication requires actual rights, author consent, current account eligibility, applicable approval and authenticated execution. Preserve valid approvals already obtained; do not invent new permission gates for ordinary private drafting.
